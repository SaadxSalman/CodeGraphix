About Focus Areas Community

Pioneering the Open-Source AI Frontier

Building the Next Generation of Intelligence.

Vectorphile is not just a platform; it's a nexus for open-source LLM research. We are a collective of researchers, engineers, and digital pioneers dedicated to democratizing access to powerful AI models. Our mission is to push the boundaries of what's possible, building transparent and reproducible large language models and the core infrastructure that powers them for a more intelligent and collaborative world.

Our Core Focus Areas

Open-Source LLMs

Developing and fine-tuning open-source models that are accessible and transparent to the global research community.

AI Tooling & Frameworks

Building the next generation of robust and intuitive tools to streamline AI development and deployment.

Vector Databases

Advancing high-performance vector databases crucial for efficient retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems.

Designing autonomous agents that can solve complex problems and automate workflows with precision.

Data & Ethics

Collaboration
